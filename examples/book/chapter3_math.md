## Entropy

The entropy of a discrete source [@shannon1948] is $H(X) = -\sum_i p_i \log_2 p_i$, measured in bits.

## Channel capacity

The capacity of a channel of bandwidth $B$ with signal-to-noise ratio $S/N$:

$$
C = B \log_2\left(1 + \frac{S}{N}\right)
$$
